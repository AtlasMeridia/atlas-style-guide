# 2026-09-25 — Minerali v6.0 review

This review of v6.0 led to the v6.1 calmer pass. Evidence came from Playwright renders of every specimen and template at their card viewports, plus 390px mobile, cream, and 中 variants.

## What held up

- The graphite editorial language is distinctive and consistent: manifesto, display, wordmark, terminal.
- The two-layer token model (raw `--mn-*` / semantic per-theme) is sound.
- The cream external-business profile reads calm and credible.
- The June streamline audit (`2026-06-30 streamline-proposal.html`) was largely executed: Literata, two themes, `--mn-` prefix, and the Minerali name.

## Rules vs practice

- **Acid.** Rule: rust carries links and accents; acid ≤15%. Practice: acid was the emphasis and link color in the blog and manifesto, and `.display em`, `code`, `.mn-slug`, selection, and focus were acid too. Kenny chose to **enforce the rule**.
- **Emphasis.** Bold and acid stacked on one word in the blog.
- **Italic.** Wordmark, monogram, palette caption, and operator lists used italic; the rule reserved it for the hero and ampersand. Resolution: the wordmark and monogram are now named exceptions, and everything else is roman.
- **Rhythm.** The README said 8px; the spacing card said 4px. Resolution: 8px rhythm with a 4px half-step.
- **Icons.** SKILL said "Lucide (CDN)"; README said hand-rolled Heroicons idiom with Lucide for prototypes. Aligned to the README.
- **Voice.** The external-business CTA said "We will decide"; the voice rule is first-person singular.
