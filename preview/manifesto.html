Build tools
that keep
their receipts.

Kenny Liu the author, and the audience
2,409 words
· 11 minutes
filed under first-principles / intent

The software I want to use keeps score for me, not against me. It remembers what I said I wanted in April and holds it up in November, not to shame me, but because I asked it to. That's not nostalgia. That's bookkeeping. The difference is whether you expect the ledger to have an opinion.

Most of what ships calls itself "intelligence" and means prediction — a system that guesses your next keystroke so you don't have to finish the thought. I want the opposite. I want a system that refuses to finish the thought, that hands it back to me with three questions in the margin, that forces the revision.

An archive is the only honest AI interface. Everything else is a magic trick a wrapper around one.
